Cento Palettes

The current palette plus five alternatives, each applied to the Cento mark, the app icon, the widget and the upcoming list. Roles are the same in every palette: Primary carries the brand, Surface is the app background, Flame is the only warm accent, and Today is a separate semantic color for the highlighted row. Contrast ratios are WCAG; text needs 4.5:1, graphics 3:1.

How to choose

  • Mezzanotte stays the strongest match for the name: the "hundred days" wish is a warm, slightly solemn thing, and a candle reads best against dark blue. Highest contrast of the set.
  • Pistacchio is the pick if you want to stand apart from every other birthday app on the store shelf while keeping the same quiet tone.
  • Lampone is the pick if instant recognition matters more than distinctiveness. It is what people expect, which is both its strength and its weakness.
  • Cobalto and Grafite move the app toward "utility" and "premium". Good if the audience is adults organizing events rather than families.
  • Pesca is the friendliest, best for a family audience, and the weakest on contrast, so its primary cannot get any lighter.
  • In dark mode every palette uses Primary as the surface and On-primary as the text, which is why those two are tested against each other above.
