Cento Mockups

Mobile screens, home-screen widgets and the web layout for Cento. The main feature is offline first and privacy first: no account, no cloud, no analytics, everything on the device and exportable. The UI is Italian by default and English as second language, chosen from the device locale or in Settings. Screens below are shown in English, with the home screen also in Italian. The list mixes birthdays, name days (onomastico) and celebrations; the person screen holds the celebration plan, gift ideas captured from the share sheet, and the gift history. Example people are placeholders. Dates assume today is Sunday 12 October 2026.

Accent, actions and selectionToday

Mobile screens

Four screens cover the whole app. The upcoming list is the home screen and is what the app opens to.

Home-screen widgets

One snapshot feeds the whole set: home screen small, medium and large, the three lock-screen families, StandBy, and the resizable Android widget. The snapshot is already translated, one entry per day, so the native widget has no strings of its own and the day count still updates at midnight without opening the app. The widget shows whichever event comes first, birthday, name day or party. Tapping opens the person.

Web app

Same screens, laid out as a three-column desktop app: navigation, the upcoming list, and the selected person. On narrow browser windows it collapses to the mobile layout. Installable as an offline PWA with IndexedDB storage.
